Senior Software Engineer

About the Role

We are looking for an experienced Senior Software Engineer to join our growing engineering team. 
You will be responsible for designing and building scalable backend services and working closely 
with our product team to deliver features that delight our customers.

This is a great opportunity for someone who wants to make a significant impact at a fast-paced 
startup while working with cutting-edge technologies.

Requirements

- 5+ years of experience in software development
- Strong proficiency in Python and/or Go
- Experience with microservices architecture
- Solid understanding of RESTful API design
- Experience with SQL databases (PostgreSQL preferred)
- Experience with cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, or Azure)
- Experience with Docker and Kubernetes
- Strong problem-solving skills
- Excellent communication skills
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or related field

Nice to Have

- Experience with GraphQL
- Knowledge of machine learning concepts
- Experience with React or Vue.js
- Familiarity with Terraform
- Open source contributions
- Experience mentoring junior developers

Responsibilities

- Design and implement scalable backend services
- Write clean, maintainable, and well-tested code
- Participate in code reviews and architectural discussions
- Collaborate with product managers to define technical requirements
- Mentor junior team members
- Contribute to improving our engineering practices

What We Offer

- Competitive salary and equity
- Remote-friendly work environment
- Health, dental, and vision insurance
- Learning and development budget
- Flexible working hours

About Us

We are a Series B startup building the next generation of developer tools. 
Our team is distributed across multiple time zones, and we value ownership, 
collaboration, and continuous learning.
